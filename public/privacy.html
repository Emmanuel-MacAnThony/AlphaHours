Alpha City Hours: privacy

Alpha City Hours is an internal timesheet for TrueLions contractors working on Alpha City. It stores the work tasks, times, output counts and proof of work that contractors log, directly or through the team’s ChatGPT assistant or Slack channel.

Data is stored in TrueLions’ Google Cloud project and is visible only to the contractor who logged it and to the managers who approve hours. Pay and rates are visible to managers only. Text may be sent to OpenAI to turn Slack reports into tasks and to write feedback. Data is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Questions or deletion requests: contact the Alpha City operations manager.
